About ASTRAVA

Your Universe. Your Destiny.

Our Story

ASTRAVA is designed to make traditional Indian knowledge easier to explore. It brings together Vedic astrology, numerology and Vaastu Shastra with AI-powered explanations in everyday language.

Our Mission

To make traditional knowledge understandable and accessible through thoughtful technology. You should not need to know complex astrological terminology to explore your own chart.

Our Approach

We explain concepts step by step, offer a choice of eight languages and distinguish calculated chart details from traditional interpretations. Our goal is guidance without fear, pressure or expensive remedies.

What ASTRAVA Offers

Birth charts with North and South Indian views, Lagna, available Panchanga and Dasha information, numerology, and combined apartment-building and home Vaastu guidance.

Astrology, numerology and Vaastu are traditional interpretive systems. They are not scientifically established methods for predicting life events. Some chart calculations are still exploratory and should not be treated as professionally validated.

How this beta handles information you enter.

The website stores your profile and recent chat history in your browser's local storage so they remain available when you return on that browser.

Your birth details, questions and relevant chat context are sent to the ASTRAVA server and an AI service to generate responses.
